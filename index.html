MRI-Based Nomogram: Neurological Recovery after Acute Cervical Spinal Cord Injury

Predicted probability of ≥1 ASIA Impairment Scale grade improvement at 6 months after surgery

MRI predictors

MSCC = [1 − a / ((b + c)/2)] × 100%, where a = narrowest cord diameter at the injury level, b and c = normal cord diameters above and below the injury level (sagittal MRI). Nomogram range 20–60%.
Craniocaudal length of intramedullary T2 hyperintensity (edema) on T2-weighted sagittal images (3.0 T MRI, 2–3 days post-injury). Entered as a dichotomy at the 48 mm threshold.
Craniocaudal length of intramedullary hemorrhage (T2 hypointensity) on T2-weighted sagittal images. Nomogram range 10–40 mm.

The authors did not publish the regression intercept or a points formula. Point weights and the points-to-probability conversion were digitised from the published nomogram (Fig. 4); the logistic fit reproduces the printed probability ticks (0.01–0.99) to within ±0.05 logit. Values are therefore approximate.

Reported model performance and source statistics
  • Cohort: 103 surgically treated acute traumatic CSCI patients (single centre, Yangzhou, China); recovery in 73 (70.9%). Modelling n = 75, internal validation n = 28.
  • C-index / AUC: 0.969 (95% CI 0.937–1.000) modelling; 0.936 (0.847–1.000) validation. Calibration by 1000 bootstrap resamples (mean absolute error 0.038).
  • Multivariable ORs with IEL dichotomised (Table 5): IEL >48 mm OR 0.01 (0.001–0.137); IHL OR 0.712 per mm (0.594–0.854); MSCC OR 0.850 per % (0.739–0.979).
  • Single-parameter ROC cut-offs: IEL 48.5 mm, IHL 21.5 mm, MSCC 43.5%.

Disclaimer

For research and educational use only. This tool is an unofficial re-implementation of a published, internally validated (not externally validated) single-centre model and is not a medical device. It does not replace clinical judgement, specialist assessment, or shared decision-making. Results outside the derivation population (surgically treated acute traumatic cervical SCI, ASIA A–C/D at baseline, MRI 2–3 days post-injury at 3.0 T) may not be valid.
